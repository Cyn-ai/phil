## Lesson → edit

Playbook, Brazil rule: extend the right-shade rule to state-level races
(Senate, governor). On those, centre the right slate at the eve valid-vote
poll mean +4, not +3. The evidence is SP's +5.6/+5.8 and DF Kicis's +3.4. In
a paired two-seat race, also pull the junior candidate of the dominant slate
toward the senior one (DF: gap 6.5 polled → 1.6 realized). When a liquid
book (>=$25k) is already pricing that right-shift end, the book outweighs a
rank-MC run that has fewer shifted draws behind it. n=2 rows from one
election, so this is provisional. The runoff (Oct 25) and any governor
second rounds are the next test.
